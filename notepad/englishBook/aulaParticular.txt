Start
The teacher shows a sentence/excerpt from a video.

Stage 1 (What was understood):
The student says what they understood.

Stage 2 (The Answer):
Only after two attempts, if the student still doesn't get it right,
the teacher reveals the text/explains the sentence.

If the student misunderstood the word ou sentence 
because of the fast pronunciation (connected speech),
use the excerpt to explain phenomena such as
linking (joining of sounds),
elision (dropping of sounds), and
reductions (words that lose their emphasis, like "gonna," "wanna").
